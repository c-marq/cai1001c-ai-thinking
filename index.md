Artificial intelligence is the most hyped technology of the decade — and the most misunderstood. One headline says it will end work; the next says it is a parlor trick. *Mind the Machine* is the third path: **understanding.** It is the textbook for **CAI1001C — AI Thinking** at Miami Dade College, and it is built to leave you able to use AI for real work, judge the claims made about it, and keep your own judgment firmly in charge.

## Why this book exists

Most writing about AI asks you to either worship the technology or fear it. This book asks you to *understand* it. Every chapter runs the same honest loop — **demystify** a piece of hype, show **how the technology actually works** in plain language, **apply it** with a real tool, and **analyze** what happened. No prior programming. No jargon walls. No assumed knowledge. Just a clear, working understanding of the most important technology of your lifetime.

## Who it is for

This is an open-enrollment survey course, and the book is written for everyone in the room: the dual-enrollment high-school student and the adult returning to school after decades away; the business, nursing, and trades majors alongside the computing students; the curious and the skeptical alike. If you have ever suspected that artificial intelligence is a subject for "other people," this book was written to prove otherwise.

## What you will be able to do

- **Explain** what AI, machine learning, deep learning, and generative AI really are — and tell genuine capability apart from hype.
- **Use** current, free AI tools — Gemini, NotebookLM, and more — on real problems from your own life and work.
- **Understand** how machines learn, see, read, talk, and create — from classifiers to large language models to generative video.
- **Think clearly about the ethics** of AI: bias, privacy, fairness, accountability, transparency, and human oversight.
- **Build** a working AI-powered application of your own — the course capstone.
