Session 3 — Digital Literacy and Data

Session 3 of 11 Week 2 Chapter 2
Monday, May 18, 2026  ·  6:00–9:20 PM
Tonight is about grounded research — working from sources you choose and trust instead of taking an AI's word for it. We will look at where data comes from and how to judge whether a source can be trusted, then put NotebookLM to work: feeding it a curated source set, asking questions, and turning its answers into study and review materials. In the second half, you and your group build a grounded notebook of your own.

Before class

What we're doing

Block A — Lecture & live walkthrough

We open with the chapter's big ideas: what digital literacy really means, how to find information and judge whether a source can be trusted, and where data comes from. Then a live walkthrough of NotebookLM — creating a notebook, adding curated sources, asking questions and checking citations, and touring the Studio panel. Use the guided demo link below to retrace the steps on your own afterward.

Block B — Discussion & breakout lab

After the break we discuss the many ways NotebookLM can be used — research, study, work, contracts, dense documents, community projects — and surface ideas from the room. Then you move into breakout groups for the lab, where each group builds a grounded notebook around a research question and tests what the tool does when pushed past its sources.
