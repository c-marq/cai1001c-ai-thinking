Before class

What we're doing

Block A — Chapter 8 + headshot demo

Chapter 8 is compressed tonight because it is a synthesis chapter — not new technical content, but the durable principles every chapter built toward. We cover the organizational reality of AI ("the hardest part is almost never the AI"), the six ethics principles, the honest state of autonomous vehicles, and then run the AI for Oceans bias-training activity for 15 minutes. Then a quick Final App Builder walk-through, and finally a live demo of a no-code professional headshot generator built end-to-end in AI Studio Build Mode from one paragraph of plain English.

Block B — Group app build (GRP09)

After the break, you move into groups. Each group picks a small real problem — a tip splitter, a unit converter, a study planner, a quick-quote tool — writes a precise app description together, builds it in AI Studio Build Mode, tests it honestly, and refines based on what broke. By 9:20 every group has a working prototype on screen. This is the practice run for your individual Final due Wednesday night.
