## What we're doing today

Last session we built a grounded notebook and pushed it to see what it would and
would not do. Tonight we put NotebookLM to work on real tasks: applying it to
everyday use cases, turning sources into study and briefing materials with the
Studio panel, and handing grounded findings to Gemini to draft something
finished. The pattern is the one from the chapter — research grounded in
NotebookLM, then drafted with Gemini.

## Block A — Recap, use cases, and the Gemini handoff

We start with a short recap of Session 3: a grounded notebook answers only from
the sources you chose, and cites them. Then we go wider — the many situations
NotebookLM fits, from a healthcare student's readings to a small-business owner's
contracts to a dense lease or policy — and we walk the workflow that carries
findings forward: research grounded in NotebookLM, then drafted with Gemini.

## Block B — Breakout lab

After the break you move into your groups for the lab. Each group picks a real
use-case scenario, builds a notebook for it, uses the Studio panel to turn the
sources into something usable, and hands the grounded findings to Gemini to draft
a finished deliverable.
