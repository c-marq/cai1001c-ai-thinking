## What we're doing today

Raw data is not insight — it is messy raw material, and turning it into something
trustworthy is real work. Tonight we climb the staircase from Chapter 3: explore
a dataset, clean it, transform it, and visualize it honestly — and we learn to
spot a chart that misleads even when every number in it is accurate. In the
second half, your group builds an honest chart of its own in Datawrapper.

## Block A — Lecture and the AI Studio demo

We open with the chapter's core idea: data is never neutral, so you explore it,
clean it, and transform it before you trust it — and the derived columns you
build are the same features a model will later learn from. Then a live
instructor demo of **Google AI Studio**, showing a developer's side of AI that
the chat box hides: a system instruction that fixes the AI's role, and
structured output that returns clean, table-ready data. (AI Studio is 18+ and a
demonstration only — your own hands-on work uses Datawrapper.)

## Block B — Breakout lab

After the break you move into groups for the lab. Each group takes a real
dataset, explores and cleans it, builds an honest chart in Datawrapper, and
stress-tests its own chart against the four ways a chart can mislead.
