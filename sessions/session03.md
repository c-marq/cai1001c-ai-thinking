## What we're doing today

Tonight is about *grounded* research — working from sources you choose and trust
instead of taking an AI's word for it. We will look at where data comes from and
how to judge whether a source can be trusted, then put NotebookLM to work:
feeding it a curated source set, asking questions, and turning its answers into
study and review materials. In the second half, you and your group build a
grounded notebook of your own.

## Block A — Lecture and live walkthrough

We open with the chapter's big ideas: what digital literacy really means, how to
find information and judge whether a source can be trusted, and where data comes
from. Then we move to a live walkthrough of NotebookLM — creating a notebook,
adding curated sources, asking questions and checking citations, and touring the
Studio panel. Use the **guided demo** link below if you want to retrace the
steps on your own afterward.

## Block B — Discussion and breakout lab

After the break we discuss the many ways NotebookLM can be used — research,
study, work, contracts, dense documents, community projects — and surface ideas
from the room. Then you move into breakout groups for the **group lab**, where
each group builds a grounded notebook around a research question and tests what
the tool does when pushed past its sources.
