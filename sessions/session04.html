Session 4 — Working with Data

Session 4 of 11 Week 2 Chapter 3

Raw data is not insight — it is messy raw material, and turning it into something trustworthy is real work. Tonight we climb the staircase from Chapter 3: explore a dataset, clean it, transform it, and visualize it honestly — and we learn to spot a chart that misleads even when every number in it is accurate. In the second half, your group builds an honest chart of its own in Datawrapper.

Before class

What we're doing

Block A — Lecture & AI Studio demo

The chapter's core idea: data is never neutral, so you explore, clean, and transform it before you trust it — and the columns you derive are the same features a model will later learn from. Then a live instructor demo of Google AI Studio, showing a developer's side of AI the chat box hides: a system instruction that fixes the AI's role, and structured output that returns clean, table-ready data. (AI Studio is 18+ and a demonstration only — your own hands-on work uses Datawrapper.)

Block B — Breakout lab

After the break you move into groups for the lab. Each group takes a real dataset, explores and cleans it, builds an honest chart in Datawrapper, and stress-tests its own chart against the four ways a chart can mislead.
