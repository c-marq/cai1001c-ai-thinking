Every tool used in *Mind the Machine*, in one place. AI products change quickly, so each entry carries the date it was last checked.

**Verified as of May 2026.** If a tool's name, price, or age policy has changed since then, treat this page as out of date and check the tool's own site.

## The age-safety rule

Every tool a student is *required* to use is **free** and usable by anyone in the class — either with no account at all, or with a free account open to ages 13 and up. A few advanced tools carry an **18+** age requirement; these are **instructor-demo only** — your instructor shows them, and you use a free, age-open tool for your own work. No student is ever shut out of an activity.
