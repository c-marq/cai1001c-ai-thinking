This map shows how the eight chapters of *Mind the Machine* align with the official **CAI1001C course competencies** and the college's general-education **Program Learning Outcomes (PLOs)**. Use it to see how each week's reading connects to what the course is accountable for teaching.

## The seven course competencies

- **C1 — The field of AI and ML.** Digital literacy, data sources and the Internet of Things, the history of AI, the AI project cycle, supervised and unsupervised learning, neural networks, and enterprise applications.
- **C2 — Ethical and legal considerations.** Computing-ethics vocabulary — algorithmic privacy, algorithmic fairness — and the ethics issues that arise as AI is developed, implemented, and administered.
- **C3 — Exploring datasets.** Data, data acquisition, visualization, and transformation; using datasets to train models.
- **C4 — Classification algorithms.** Linear classifiers, support vector machines, k-nearest neighbors, decision trees, and neural networks.
- **C5 — Computer vision.** Image segmentation, object and motion detection, object classification, and facial recognition.
- **C6 — Natural language processing.** Machine translation, sentiment analysis, deep learning for language, and speech recognition and synthesis.
- **C7 — Robotic sensing and manipulation.** Robotics, sensing, human–robot interaction, navigation and path planning, reinforcement learning, and autonomous vehicles.

## The four program learning outcomes

- **PLO1** — Use quantitative analytical skills to evaluate and process numerical data.
- **PLO2** — Solve problems using critical and creative thinking and scientific reasoning.
- **PLO3** — Demonstrate knowledge of ethical thinking and its application to society.
- **PLO4** — Use computer and emerging technologies effectively.

## A note on ethics

Competency **C2 — ethical and legal considerations** is the one competency that does not sit in a single chapter. Ethics runs through the whole book — bias in Chapter 1, privacy and sources in Chapter 2, honest charts in Chapter 3, fairness in Chapter 4, surveillance and consent in Chapter 5, manipulation in Chapter 6, deepfakes and provenance in Chapter 7 — and is gathered into a full synthesis in Chapter 8. Every chapter also carries an **Ethics Check** callout. C2 is therefore listed formally with Chapter 8, but it is taught all term.
