GRP04 — Build an Honest Chart

4 points Group of 3–4 ~40 minutes
Session 4 · Chapter 3: Working with Data  |  Featured tool: Datawrapper  |  Due: end of class
💡 WHAT THIS LAB IS Tonight your group takes a real dataset from someone's line of work — a job, a field of study, a community role — and walks it up the Chapter 3 staircase: explore it, clean it, and turn it into one honest chart in Datawrapper. The goal is honest work, not a fancy chart. Datawrapper is free, runs in the browser, and needs no account.

Group members & roles

Every member picks one role. All roles contribute — no coding is required for any of them.

RoleMember name
Lead Driver — runs Datawrapper on the shared screen
Data Interpreter — leads explore & clean, owns the cleaning decision
Presenter — delivers the share-out to the class
QA Reviewer — stress-tests the chart and fills this worksheet

1Choose a dataset from someone's line of work

✅ DO THIS — 10 min As a group, pick one real, small dataset tied to a member's job, studies, or community role. It needs at least one date or category column and one number column. Real examples by field:
  • Healthcare — monthly patient-visit counts; supplies used per week.
  • Business / retail — weekly sales; orders per product; foot traffic by day.
  • Skilled trades — job durations; material costs per month.
  • Community / nonprofit — a food pantry's monthly demand; event attendance.
  • Student life — study hours per week; club attendance; monthly expenses.
No real dataset on hand? Type a small honest one from memory (10–15 rows), or use a small public dataset.
💡 WHY ARE WE DOING THIS? A chart only matters when it answers a real question someone actually has. Starting from a member's own work means the question is real — and the person who knows the data can tell the group whether a strange value is an error or a true story.

2Explore and clean it together

✅ DO THIS — 10 min Apply Sections 3.1 and 3.2. As a group: note the dataset's size (rows and columns), confirm what each column is, and hunt for at least one problem — a missing value, an error, a duplicate, or an inconsistent format. Decide together how to handle it, and write the decision down.
Type:
⚠️ COMMON MISTAKE Deleting every row with a blank "to be safe." A cleaning choice is a judgment call — if the rows you drop all belong to one group, you have quietly erased that group from the chart. Decide on purpose, and record the decision.
🛑 STOP AND CHECK Before building: your group can state the dataset's size, you have found one real problem, and you have written down your handling decision. If the decision isn't written, write it now.

3Build the chart in Datawrapper

✅ DO THIS — 10 min
  • Go to datawrapper.de and create a new chart — no account needed.
  • Paste or upload your cleaned data. Check that each column is read correctly (date, number, category).
  • Choose the chart type that fits your question (Section 3.4): bar to compare, line for change over time, scatter for a relationship.
  • Give it an honest title that states the point — "Patient visits rose 22% over the spring," not "Visits Chart."
  • Confirm the axis starts at zero and the labels are clear.

4Stress-test your own chart

✅ DO THIS — 5 min Turn Section 3.5 on your own chart. Check it honestly against all four tricks — and fix anything that fails before you submit.
Misleading trickIs our chart clear of it?
Truncated axis — does the axis start at zero?
Cherry-picked range — does it show the full relevant span?
Wrong chart type — does the type fit the question?
Missing context — do the numbers have something to compare to?
🛑 STOP AND CHECK Could anyone reasonably accuse your chart of misleading? If yes, fix it now — an honest chart is the whole point of tonight.

5Prepare your share-out

✅ DO THIS — 5 min Your Presenter gives the class a 60-second report. Fill this in together.

What to submit

💡 ONE WORKSHEET PER GROUP This completed worksheet is your submission. It must include your finished chart, one sentence on the data problem you found and your decision, and one sentence on the chart's main point.
✅ HOW TO SUBMIT In your browser, choose File → Print → Save as PDF. Confirm your chart screenshot is not cut off, then upload the PDF to the GRP04 assignment in Canvas. One submission per group, with every member's name in the roles table. Due by the end of class.

Troubleshooting

ProblemFix
Datawrapper reads our number column as text.Check for stray characters — a currency symbol, a comma, a space. Clean those out of the data and re-paste.
Our dates won't line up on the axis.Inconsistent date formats. Make them all one format (Section 3.2) before pasting — Datawrapper charts what you give it.
The chart looks flat and uninteresting.That may be the honest truth of the data. Do not truncate the axis to manufacture drama — a flat result is a real result.
The group can't agree on a chart type.Go back to your Step 1 question. Comparing categories → bar. Change over time → line. A relationship between two numbers → scatter.
A pasted screenshot won't show when printing.Click inside the dashed box first, then paste. If it still clips, paste a smaller image.
