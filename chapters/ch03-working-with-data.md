**What this chapter will cover**

How to explore, clean, and transform raw data into something useful. How to visualize data honestly — and how to spot a chart that misleads. The workflow that turns a dataset into a working model.

*Featured hands-on tool:* Datawrapper (with a Google AI Studio demonstration).

Every chapter ends the same way: five review questions, a discussion question,
an instructor-led walkthrough, a breakout-group lab where you apply the tool to
your own idea, and an optional take-home that adds ethics and bias to the
reflection.
