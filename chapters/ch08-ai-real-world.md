**What this chapter will cover**

How organizations adopt AI and judge their readiness for it. Robotics, sensing, and autonomous systems. A synthesis of the ethics threads running through every chapter. And the course capstone — designing and building your own AI-powered application.

*Featured hands-on tool:* the course capstone project.

Every chapter ends the same way: five review questions, a discussion question,
an instructor-led walkthrough, a breakout-group lab where you apply the tool to
your own idea, and an optional take-home that adds ethics and bias to the
reflection.
