**What this chapter will cover**

How a computer represents an image as numbers. How machines classify images, detect objects and motion, segment a scene, and recognize faces — and the real risks of that last one. How AI generates brand-new images from a description.

*Featured hands-on tool:* Hugging Face Spaces (with a Nano Banana demonstration).

Every chapter ends the same way: five review questions, a discussion question,
an instructor-led walkthrough, a breakout-group lab where you apply the tool to
your own idea, and an optional take-home that adds ethics and bias to the
reflection.
