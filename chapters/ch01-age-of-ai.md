**What this chapter will cover**

What artificial intelligence, machine learning, and deep learning actually are — and how they differ from ordinary software. The honest history of AI, including its booms and its "winters." Supervised versus unsupervised learning. The AI project cycle that every AI system follows.

*Featured hands-on tool:* Gemini.

Every chapter ends the same way: five review questions, a discussion question,
an instructor-led walkthrough, a breakout-group lab where you apply the tool to
your own idea, and an optional take-home that adds ethics and bias to the
reflection.
