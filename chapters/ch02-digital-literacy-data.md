**What this chapter will cover**

What it means to be digitally literate. How to find information and judge whether a source can be trusted. Where data comes from, how it is collected, and the role of the Internet of Things. How AI can ground its answers in sources you choose.

*Featured hands-on tool:* NotebookLM.

Every chapter ends the same way: five review questions, a discussion question,
an instructor-led walkthrough, a breakout-group lab where you apply the tool to
your own idea, and an optional take-home that adds ethics and bias to the
reflection.
